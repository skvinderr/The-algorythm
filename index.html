About PTSC

Where passion meets programming

Our Mission

To create a collaborative environment where students can develop their programming skills, work on real-world projects, and prepare for tech careers.

Our Vision

To be the premier student-run programming community that fosters innovation, technical excellence, and professional growth.

Our Values

Collaboration, Continuous Learning, Innovation, Inclusivity, and Hands-on Experience.

Our Journey

2018

Club Founded

Started with just 5 members meeting weekly to work on small coding projects.

2019

First Hackathon

Organized our first 24-hour hackathon with over 50 participants.

2020

Virtual Transition

Successfully moved all activities online, expanding our reach to 200+ members.

2024

Industry Partnerships

Established partnerships with 5 tech companies for workshops and internships.

2025

500+ Members

Reached our milestone of 500 active members across multiple universities.
